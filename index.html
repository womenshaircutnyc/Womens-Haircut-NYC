A Luxury Salon Experience in Manhattan

Finding a professional hairstylist you trust in New York is not easy. At our Upper East Side salon, each visit starts with a relaxed haircut consultation. We talk about your lifestyle, your hair type, how much time you spend styling, and the look you want. Then we shape a plan that suits you.

Our luxury salon experience is about care and comfort. You will find a calm space, skilled stylists and honest advice. Whether you want a precision haircut, a soft layered haircut, a short haircut or a long haircut, we focus on clean lines, healthy ends and movement that looks good every day.

Haircuts and Styling for Women

Our women's haircut services include hair trimming, hair shaping, and a full haircut and style finish. A good cut grows out gracefully, so we study your face shape, growth patterns and density before we begin. Many clients enjoy a haircut transformation, and we are happy to guide you toward modern haircut trends that still feel like you.

Prefer an event ready finish? Our blowout styling gives smooth, bouncy, long lasting results for work, dinners, weddings and weekends.

Color, Highlights and Extensions

Women's hair coloring is both art and chemistry. We offer women's hair highlights, soft blends, rich all over color and hair gloss and shine treatments that bring back depth and luster. If you want extra length or volume, our women's hair extensions blend naturally with your own hair.

Healthy Hair Care You Can See

Beautiful hair begins with healthy hair. Our hair care services include healthy hair treatment, keratin smoothing for frizz control, and personal product advice for home. We want your hair to look polished on the day you leave and stay manageable for weeks.
